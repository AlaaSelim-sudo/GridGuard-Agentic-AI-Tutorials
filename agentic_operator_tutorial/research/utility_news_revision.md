# Eversource and NYPA: revision evidence

Reviewed through 17 October 2026. The companion JSON supplies merge-ready sources **N07–N11**, verified full-page evidence, short exact excerpts, claim boundaries and **two proposed slides**. Shared content and source maps were not edited.

**Eversource:** its [official 5 October engineering posting (N07)](https://jobs.eversource.com/job/east-berlin/distribution-system-modeling-and-automation-engineer-hybrid-schedule/7774/101576695504) directly requests agentic MATLAB skills in a real distribution-planning role. This is stronger evidence of engineering intent than generic market commentary. It establishes a capability requirement, not a live agent platform. The [2026 proxy (N08)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/72741/000110465926036077/tm261423-1_def14a.htm) provides general AI and planned human-oversight context. [MathWorks’ named Eversource report (N11)](https://www.mathworks.com/company/newsroom/eversource-energy-collaborates-with-mathworks-to-incorporate-renewable-energy-sources-into-system-planning-process-using-probabilistic-load-flow-automation.html) verifies the existing MATLAB/Synergi numerical-automation foundation. Its simulation-time result must remain attributed to that conventional automation.

**NYPA:** use the [original Ataccama session report (N09)](https://www.ataccama.com/webinar/on-demand-trust-driven-medallion-architecture) with named NYPA Data Quality Lead Saptaparna Gupta. It supports a concrete discussion of data quality for agent use. It does not establish NYPA deployment of the demonstrated agent. The [live event listing (N10)](https://www.ataccama.com/events/trust-driven-medallion-architecture) currently gives 13 October 2026; the on-demand page gives 14 October. An older indexed excerpt gave 6 October, so avoid copying that stale date.

The research did **not verify an Eversource or NYPA production grid-control agent deployment**. The proposed labels therefore distinguish Eversource’s engineering capability-building from NYPA’s data-quality implementation and the session’s separate agent demo. PJM, CAISO and Duke should continue to anchor the claims about named operational agent use.

Do not infer modern agents from historical C3/Board customer stories with refreshed product boilerplate. Do not count a 2027 session as a completed 2026 event. The older NYPA board packet was not promoted to a reference because current direct retrieval failed. Those search leads and their limits are preserved in the JSON.
