# Power-only introduction: news and case evidence

Reviewed 17 October 2026. The companion JSON contains six proposed news slides, source-map additions N01–N06, source locators, operational boundaries and original visual opportunities. The shared source map and slide content were not edited.

Lead with **PJM/HyperQ, CAISO/OATI and Duke/AWS**: three named U.S. grid-engineering workflows. Continue with **Siemens PSS E** as a product unveiling and **National Grid** as a company disclosure of current project-assurance use. Iberdrola supplies a sixth adoption story only if useful; its portfolio total deliberately combines production and development. These are different evidence categories, so a common “deployed platforms” heading would overstate them.

The strongest new example is [Tapestry’s June field report (N01)](https://www.tapestryenergy.com/en/projects/field-notes-hyperq-deployment-in-pjm-s-cycle-1). It is unusually useful for teaching because it separates computational processing from human determination and publishes both median and p95 latency. Keep its application, document-bundle and criterion-assessment denominators separate. Omit its historical manual-versus-machine speedup headline: the source acknowledges an unmatched comparison across different workflow boundaries. It reports no accuracy denominator. Its future roadmap mentions ADK; that does not establish present use of ADK.

The [technical white paper (N02)](https://www.tapestryenergy.com/en/projects/how-agentic-ai-can-help-grid-operators-speed-up-interconnection) offers better teaching visuals than a generic agent illustration: Figure A demonstrates the application report, Figure B shows coordinated agents, and Figure C shows exact evidence selection. The direct GIF/PNG URLs and credits are in the JSON. Describe them as product demonstrations, not photographs of PJM’s live control room. [PJM’s own March progress report (N06)](https://insidelines.pjm.com/connected/) independently confirms the earlier testing phase, without the later supplier metrics.

[Siemens’ May release (N03)](https://press.siemens.com/global/en/pressrelease/siemens-gridscale-x-redefines-system-operations-and-agentic-transmission-planning) places a platform-adoption paragraph beside a new agentic-product paragraph. Preserve that distinction: Alliander’s application migration does not prove it deployed the new PSS E agents. [National Grid’s results transcript (N04)](https://www.nationalgrid.com/document/577036/download), PDF page 10, supports actual stage-gate project assurance but supplies no framework or agent-specific outcome. [Iberdrola’s announcement (N05)](https://www.iberdrola.com/press-room/news/detail/iberdrola-accelerates-adoption-agentic-ai-support-talent-professionals) requires the same discipline about mixed maturity and general AI versus agent-specific results.

The JSON also preserves weaker leads without promoting them into measured deployment cases: Powercor’s official AWS conference sessions, an open-source AWS field-safety reference application, Hitachi HMAX, GE Vernova operator-assistance discussion and Schneider’s major-event offering. Existing AES audit material is real but less central to power-system engineering than the three U.S. workflow anchors. Pipeline-only and biomedical examples should leave this route.
