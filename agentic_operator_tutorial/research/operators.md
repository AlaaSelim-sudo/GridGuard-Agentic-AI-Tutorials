# Operator facts and tutorial uses

Evidence checked 17 October 2026. Full URLs, dates, locators, and caveats are in `operators.json`.

- **O01 — PJM, 16 April 2026:** The operator team covers 24/7 operation, balances supply/demand, monitors transmission overloads, and plans next-day needs. PJM reports eight weeks of training yearly, including simulation and grid exercises. These are team coverage and PJM program facts, not universal individual shift rules.
- **O02 — PJM, 11 August 2025, CB006 Revision 9:** Job positions include Generation Dispatcher, Power Dispatcher, Reliability Engineer, Master Coordinator, and Shift Supervisor. Duties span balancing/reserves, transmission security/voltage/outages, next-day analysis, forecasting/scheduling, and oversight. Two control rooms operate as one team with shared EMS, dispatch tools, and electronic logging. Job positions do not map one-to-one onto NERC functional titles.
- **O03 — CAISO, 20 February 2025:** Its expanded simulator room has 18 consoles. The reported operator rotation has one training week every six weeks; simulations can run for eight hours. Attribute these figures specifically to CAISO's 2025 description.
- **O04 — Duke Energy, 2 August 2022:** The operator profile describes combining weather, plant and line status, demand, and studies using real-time data. Treat the featured reliability coordinator's region and duties as historical local context.

Suggested additions: one slide on real operator duties, one on PJM desk roles, and one mapping tasks to bounded tool use. For example, an agent can draft a source-linked briefing, screen outage records for missing information, retrieve applicable procedures, invoke a validated study tool, or support a simulator debrief. Label all these mappings **illustrative operator workflows** unless separately supported by a disclosed deployment case. The operator owns the decision and required coordination.

Do not call the 24/7 team schedule an individual shift length. Do not generalize PJM's five job positions, two control rooms, or annual training program to every U.S. utility. Do not treat CAISO's training facility as evidence that agents already perform the training.
