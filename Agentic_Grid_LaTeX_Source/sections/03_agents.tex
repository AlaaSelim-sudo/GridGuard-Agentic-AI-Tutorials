\begin{frame}[t]{The Llama contract separates a tool choice from a control action}
\deepdive
\checkpoint{A2}{Match each language response to a permitted transition in the trace.}
\small
Let $r$ denote a workflow role, $\mathcal C$ the validated task, $\mathcal E_r$ the available evidence, and $\mathcal A_r$ that role's allowed choices.
\[
 d_r=\operatorname{Llama}(\mathcal C,\mathcal E_r,\mathcal A_r),
 \qquad d_r\in\mathcal A_r.
\]
Here $d_r$ is a \textbf{next-step choice}. Numerical tools produce $u=(a,b)$, where $p_g=aS_g$, $q_g=bS_g$ and $S_g$ is inverter $g$'s rating.
\par\smallskip
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{.17\linewidth}p{.43\linewidth}p{.34\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule Role & Permitted response & What it triggers \\
\midrule
Planning & \texttt{VERIFY\_SAC\_FIRST} or \texttt{REVIEW} & Check the supplied SAC point, or stop \\
Recovery & \texttt{USE\_MPC\_PLAN} or \texttt{REVIEW} & Check the eligible MPC point, or stop \\
Explanation & Focus on action, evidence or model limits & Choose the focus of the evidence card \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\par\smallskip
Each reply contains \texttt{choice} and \texttt{reason}. Schema validation checks their form; deterministic checks decide numerical acceptance. Neither creates human authorization.
\sources{Implemented interface 1.1: \texttt{src/continuous\_workflow.py}. Tool/observation reasoning: Yao et al., \url{https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629}.}
\note{Use this as the contract-reading bridge before the paired experiment. Define every symbol aloud: r is a named role, C is the accepted task, E is the information already available, and A is the finite menu. The equation describes the implemented interface, not an optimality theorem. The action u contains shared real-valued active and reactive participation factors; the numerical SAC or MPC tool produces these values. The actual explanation enum values are REVIEW\_ACTION, REVIEW\_EVIDENCE and REVIEW\_MODEL\_LIMITS. In this current interface, REVIEW\_EVIDENCE is only an explanation focus, distinct from the stop choice REVIEW. The system validates JSON keys and the permitted enum, but a well-formed answer can still be wrong in meaning. The service or schema failure fallback is REVIEW. Current Llama settings are llama3.1:8b, temperature zero and seed 23; these do not establish semantic correctness or field reliability.}
\end{frame}
