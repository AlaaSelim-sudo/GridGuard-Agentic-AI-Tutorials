\subsection*{Using the case and source guide}
\textbf{Format:} 50 self-contained concept slides and three what-if discussions. No software or account is required.\par
\textbf{Learning route:} Understand the problem, change one condition, follow the agent request and numerical result, then identify the operator decision.\par
\textbf{Evidence:} Numerical figures are prepared results. Recorded language-model replies retain their own case inputs and scope. A different recorded study is explicitly labeled. These examples do not demonstrate field deployment or general performance gains.\par
\textbf{Practice afterward:} The participant lab guide and notebooks are separate optional practice material. Their setup and cell labels are not needed to understand this presentation.\par
\textbf{Credits:} External Stanford material retains its credits. Full source and calculation provenance are collected here; the authors' own case slides have no source footer.\par
